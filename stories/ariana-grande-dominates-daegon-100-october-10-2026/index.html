Ariana Grande sweeps the Daegon 100 top three as “petal” returns to No. 1

The October 10 chart is a full-spectrum Ariana week: three songs lead the Daegon 100, petal tops albums, and Grande returns to No. 1 on Artist 50.

By Daegon Charts Editorial · October 13, 2026

Ariana Grande controls all three of Daegon’s flagship rankings on the chart dated October 10, 2026. “hate that i made you love me” rises from No. 3 to No. 1 on the Daegon 100, “kiss me” holds at No. 2 and “petal” climbs from No. 6 to No. 3. That gives Grande the entire top three in songs while the parent album petal rises 3–1 on Top 100 Albums and Grande moves 2–1 on Artist 50.

1–2–3Ariana Grande occupies every position in the Daegon 100 top three this week.

The No. 1 is not a brief spike. “hate that i made you love me” reaches its nineteenth chart week and records a twelfth week at the summit. Its rise is driven by a balanced week across the three signals shown in the Daegon table: 10,000 sales, 32,000 streams and 87,008 airplay, producing 181 chart points.

Jão remains the strongest challenger

Jão’s “Aurora” rises to No. 4, while “Catedral” sits at No. 6. Memórias Póstumas is No. 5 on albums and Jão is No. 7 on Artist 50. The result makes him the highest-ranked artist outside the Ariana sweep in the song top five and keeps a Brazilian release near the center of all three flagship charts.

A sharp reset below the top four

Sienna Spiro’s “Great Expectation,” last week’s No. 1, drops to No. 5 in its second chart week. Taylor Swift’s “Patient Zero” falls 4–7, while Olivia Dean’s “So Easy (To Fall In Love)” returns to the top 10 at No. 10 after 36 weeks on the chart.

Catalog songs keep moving beside new releases

The lower half of the list demonstrates why the Daegon 100 is not only a release-week chart. Rihanna’s “Umbrella” re-enters at No. 26, Madonna’s “Music” rises to No. 33, Michael Jackson’s “Beat It” climbs to No. 61 and Britney Spears’ “Toxic” re-enters at No. 90. Current releases and older recordings therefore compete in the same weekly frame when consumption returns.

Albums tell the same story at a wider scale

petal rises to No. 1 for an eighth week at the top, followed by Taylor Swift’s The Life of a Showgirl and Olivia Rodrigo’s you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love. Victoria Monét debuts Frequency Of Love at No. 9, while Rebecca Black’s Age of the Exhibitionist starts at No. 31.

The Artist 50 consolidates those signals rather than simply repeating the song list. Grande leads, Swift slips to No. 2, Rodrigo jumps 20–3 and Rihanna climbs 26–5 as several older albums and songs return simultaneously.
