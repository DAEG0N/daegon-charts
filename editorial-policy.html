Standards

Editorial Policy

Daegon Charts is an independent music chart project and publication. Editorial pages are built to add context to the chart archive, not to manufacture volume for search engines or advertising.

Original reporting and analysis

When a story cites a Daegon chart position, the underlying Daegon weekly archive is the primary source. External facts should be checked against reliable primary or established sources before publication.

Historical reconstruction

Reconstructed charts are models based on surviving evidence and a published framework. They are not presented as direct measurements that existed at the time. Material methodology changes should be reflected in the methodology page.

Corrections

Factual errors, duplicated chart identities and incorrect credits should be corrected when verified. Significant editorial corrections should be reflected in the article’s updated date or correction note.

AI assistance

AI may assist with research organization, code, draft structure and large-scale data checks. It is not treated as an independent source. Published editorial content should be checked against the underlying chart data and reliable references.

Advertising and independence

Advertising does not determine chart positions or editorial conclusions. Sponsored content, if introduced, should be clearly labeled and kept separate from the chart methodology.

Authorship

Stories produced under the site’s editorial workflow are credited to Daegon Charts Editorial. The project maintains separate methodology, privacy and contact pages so readers can understand how data and editorial decisions are handled.

Daegon Charts is an independent music chart and historical archive.
